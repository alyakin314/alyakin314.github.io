The machine and its matrix

A prepared state and knob axis on a Bloch sphere, two lamps with editable labels and probabilities, and the Hermitian matrix those settings define.

  • σz, then σx, then σy. Same labels, knob rotated. The ones jump off-diagonal, then go imaginary.
  • Identity, then spin the knob. All four entries stay frozen. Both lamps read the same number, so direction means nothing.
  • Trace and determinant. The sum and the product of your two labels. Turn the knob and they hold; retype a label and they move.
  • The check that matters. The observed mean converges on ⟨A⟩, which is just ⟨ψ|A|ψ⟩. Experiment on the left, arithmetic on the right.
