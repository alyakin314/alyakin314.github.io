The Hermitian matrix

A knob direction and two dial numbers, and the two by two Hermitian matrix they produce, with its trace, determinant and eigenvalues.

knob tilt0°
knob swing0°
dial labels
eigenvalues 1, −1
  • Turn the knob. The entries rearrange. Trace, determinant and eigenvalues all hold still.
  • Retype a label. Trace, determinant and eigenvalues move. The knob has not shifted.
  • Where the four live. Two eigenvalues and two angles for the eigenbasis. The four matrix entries look like four numbers, but Hermiticity forces the diagonal real and pins the lower-left to the conjugate of the upper-right.
  • Identity. Set both labels equal and the knob stops mattering. A repeated eigenvalue leaves no eigenbasis to point.
